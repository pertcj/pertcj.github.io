I am a PhD researcher in the [Department of Computing](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/computing/) at Imperial College London, advised by [Professor Alessandra Russo](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/people/a.russo) and [Dr Pranava Madhyastha](https://pmadhyastha.github.io/). My research sits at the intersection of **controlled inference, post-training, neuro-symbolic learning, and reasoning in large language models**. I'm currently working on:

- **Provably correct, efficient decoding**: decoding methods that give language models formal correctness guarantees on their outputs and are parameter- and token-efficient, with System 2-style reasoning built into the decoding process itself.
- **Post-training for reasoning**: methods that guide models toward better reasoning trajectories, more deliberate, correct, and controllable.
- **Embodied and multimodal agents**: agents and environments for embodied reasoning, and vision-language models (VLMs).

Between October 2025 and January 2026 I was a Research Scientist at **Google DeepMind**, embedded with the Gemini team, where I contributed to frontier model research and development, including work on **DeepThink**, spanning model capabilities and the design of new evaluation methodologies. I also co-led two projects bridging DeepMind researchers with Imperial faculty and graduate students. Since June 2024 I have also been a Research Assistant at Imperial, where I serve as tech lead on an Imperial Policy Forum project with DSIT and the AI Security Institute (AISI) focused on LLM agents, build and lead research collaborations with chemists and scientists on molecular design, drug discovery, and multi-step retrosynthesis, and help maintain Imperial's GPU compute clusters as HPC Administrator.

Before Imperial, I completed an MSc in Artificial Intelligence (Distinction) and a BSc in Computer Science (First-Class Honours) at King's College London, where I worked with [Dr Yali Du](https://yalidu.github.io/) on transformer-based online reinforcement learning agents inspired by global workspace theory.
