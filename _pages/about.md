I am a final-year PhD student and Teaching Scholar in the Department of Computing at Imperial College London. I am supervised by [Alessandra Russo](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/people/a.russo) and [Dalal Alrajeh](https://www.doc.ic.ac.uk/~da04/). My research is on using machine learning to scale formal verification methods. With industry experience including an internship at Epic Games with a focus on using LLMs and VLMs for large-scale automatic data labelling and captioning for content moderation and search, I am experienced in both academic research and industrial work.

Between October 2025 and January 2026, I was a Researcher at Imperial as part of the [AI for Math Initiative](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-deepmind/ai-for-math/) supported by Google DeepMind and Google.org. I was also a Lecturer for Symbolic Reasoning, a 2nd year undergraduate course, between 2023 and 2026 Before Imperial, I completed an MSc in Scientific Computing and Data Analysis and a BSc in Mathematics and Physics at Durham University.
