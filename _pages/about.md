I am a PhD researcher in the [Department of Computing](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/computing/) at Imperial College London, advised by [Professor Alessandra Russo](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/people/a.russo) and [Dr Pranava Madhyastha](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/people/pranava.madhyastha). My research sits at the intersection of **controlled inference, post-training, and reasoning in large language models**, drawing on neuro-symbolic methods to induce control in LLMs at both the reasoning and generation level &mdash; work aimed at making models more deliberate and reliable ("System 2" thinking). I run experiments across code generation, agent planning, semantic parsing, mathematics, and logical reasoning, and my broader interests include foundation models, reinforcement learning, neuro-symbolic learning, and reasoning in neural networks.

Between October 2025 and January 2026 I was a Research Scientist at **Google DeepMind**, embedded with the Gemini team, where I contributed to frontier model research spanning model capabilities and the design of new evaluation methodologies (including work on DeepThink), and co-led two projects bridging DeepMind researchers with Imperial faculty and graduate students. Since June 2024 I have also been a Research Assistant at Imperial, where I serve as tech lead on an Imperial Policy Forum project with DSIT and the AI Security Institute (AISI) focused on LLM agents, build and lead research collaborations with chemists and scientists on molecular design, drug discovery, and multi-step retrosynthesis, and help maintain Imperial's GPU compute clusters as HPC Administrator.

Before Imperial, I completed an MSc in Artificial Intelligence (Distinction) and a BSc in Computer Science (First-Class Honours) at King's College London, where I worked with Dr Yali Du on transformer-based online reinforcement learning agents inspired by global workspace theory.
