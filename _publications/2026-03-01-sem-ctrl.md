Awarded a Journal-to-Conference certification and presented at **ICML 2026** in Seoul (July 2026).

This paper introduces SEM-CTRL, an approach to semantically controlled decoding for large language models, aimed at inducing control over model outputs at generation time to improve the validity and reliability of generated text.
